Methodology: `SEO_brief/on-page-seo.md` and `CRM/operations/sops/seo-optimisation.md`. Site fully launched 2026-04-26 with mega-navbar across all pages. Production domain: `https://charmexcavation.com.au/` (non-www).

## Scope

10 pages: `index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `gallery.html`, `contact.html`, plus 5 service detail pages under `services/`: `civil-drainage`, `excavation-earthmoving`, `landscaping-rural`, `retaining-walls`, `site-preparation-demolition`.

## Critical findings

1. **Zero SEO infrastructure.** No canonicals, no Open Graph, no Twitter cards, no geo meta, no JSON-LD schema (LocalBusiness, Service, Organization, BreadcrumbList - none), no sitemap.xml, no robots.txt anywhere.
2. **Homepage missing meta description.** All other pages have one; index.html does not.
3. **No skip-to-content link or `<main>` wrapper** on any page. Accessibility miss site-wide.
4. **Smart apostrophes (`'`)** in some descriptions. Should normalise to straight apostrophes for consistency.
5. **No fabricated `aggregateRating` anywhere** - good. We need none either: Charm has no verified review count yet.
