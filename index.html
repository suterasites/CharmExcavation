About Charm Excavation

Local. Hands-on.
No shortcuts.

Charm Excavation is a Keysborough-based, owner-operated earthmoving outfit serving Melbourne's South East.

Every job gets the operator on the ground, not a rotating crew. That means tight quotes, clean sites, and the same person on day one and day done. From bulk cuts to final trim, drainage to rural earthworks, we handle the dirt so your build can start on time.
