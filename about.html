Our Story

Local. Hands-on.
No shortcuts.

Dylan has been running machines for ten years and Charm Excavation has been going three of those, from a home base in Keysborough. The rig is owner-operated, so the same person quoting the job is the one on the machine.

The work has always been honest: tight quotes, clean sites, finishing what gets started. No dressing it up, no overpromises. If it's not the right job for us, we'll say so. If we can help, the quote lands fast and we turn up when we said we would.

We run across Melbourne's South East, from residential cuts in Bentleigh to civil and drainage work across Dandenong, Frankston, the Mornington Peninsula and everything in between.
